Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I choose my spice level?

We offer 4 spice levels: Non-Spicy Bone Broth, Mild, Medium, and Extra Spicy. You can choose your broth level when ordering.

2. Do you offer vegetarian options?

Yes! We have a fresh vegetable and mushroom broth along with over 30 types of greens, tofu, and noodles.

3. Do I need to book in advance for events?

Walk-ins are welcome, but for groups of 5 or more or for the Spicy Malatang Challenge, we recommend sending us a message above.

4. Is parking available at the restaurant?

Yes, free motorbike and car parking is available right in front of FireBowl Malatang.
